  The Council of the Faeir, led by Aurvang the Ancient, is a confederation of faeir circles from across Ermis, each governed by its own faeir lord or lady. This council is characterized by a collaborative and consensus-driven decision-making process, eschewing formal voting structures in favor of a more organic, discussion-based approach. Reflecting their Chaotic Neutral alignment, the council prioritizes the preservation of the natural order and the independence of faeir communities from external influences, particularly the authoritarian Dyarchy's expansionist policies.

  Located in Thalassia, the council's base, the faeir possess unique nature-based magic closely tied to the land of Ermis. Their magical network, consisting of enchanted trees and stone circles, facilitates communication and transportation across their domains. While they cooperate with like-minded druidic and shamanic groups, the faeir generally maintain an isolationist stance. Their main adversary is the Dyarchy, whose resource exploitation and territorial expansion threaten the faeir's autonomy. The council strives to avoid direct conflict, focusing instead on defending their territories and keeping the balance of nature intact in Ermis.
