organization: The Gnostari operates under a hierarchical structure, led by Metagnostic Sibylla Eudocia. The order is divided into various ranks, with each rank responsible for specific duties ranging from recruitment to training and enforcement.
goals: To recruit and train psychics, maintain psychic order, and serve the Dyarchy
resources: The Gnostari has access to state-of-the-art psychic training facilities, extensive libraries of esoteric knowledge, and a vast network of informants and scouts to identify potential psychics.
alignment: Lawful (dedicated to maintaining order and control over psychic abilities)
allies: The Gnostari's main ally is The Dyarchy, providing them with resources and authority. They also collaborate with various psychic academies and institutions for training and research purposes.
enemies: The Gnostari considers the Haim Collective and Fair as adversaries, mainly because these groups shelter psychics who resist or evade Gnostari's control.
hooks: |-
  1. A mission to secretly recruit a powerful psychic from a remote village.
  2. Conflict with rogue psychics from the Haim Collective or Fair.
  3. A quest to retrieve or protect an ancient psychic artifact of immense power.
overview: The Gnostari is a prestigious psychic order serving The Dyarchy, devoted to discovering, training, and harnessing psychic talents for the betterment and protection of society. They are renowned for their strict discipline and rigorous training programs.
