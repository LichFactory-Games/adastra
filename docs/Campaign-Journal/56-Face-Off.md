We made peace with the Mesho'a party and agreed to a mutually beneficial course of action. We would pool our resources to find the via astra together and get it out the pyramid. In return we guaranteed safe passage out of the desert to the other Mesho'ans. 

We destroyed a nest of desert centipedes in the next room and then, after encountering the body of another fallen comrade of the Mesho'an expedition, made our way through the rest of the level. 

We discovered a hidden room, only to be dismayed that it contained shrieking dust devils and oozing sand.
