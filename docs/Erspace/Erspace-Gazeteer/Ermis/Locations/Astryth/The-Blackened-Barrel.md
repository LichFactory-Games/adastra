overview: |-
  The Blackened Barrel in Astryth is a notorious establishment, known for its understated luxury. The interior is dimly lit by flickering candles and shaded lanterns, casting long shadows across rich wooden furnishings and deep burgundy tapestries that adorn the walls.

  Velik, the enigmatic leader of the Five Knives, is often found here, overseeing the syndicate's activities from a quiet alcove. He is a master of disguise and manipulation, and his presence adds an air of tension and intrigue to the establishment. His loyal enforcers, known as the "Bladehands," discreetly patrol the tavern, ensuring that the Blackened Barrel remains a secure hub for the Five Knives' operations.

  Despite its criminal ties, the Blackened Barrel is also a place of information and connections. Whispers of intrigue, rumors of hidden treasures, and hushed conversations about city politics can be overheard in the corners of the tavern. Patrons must always be cautious about what they say and to whom they speak, as the walls have ears, and secrets have a way of changing hands in this shadowy establishment.
